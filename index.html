Working principles

Built for clarity and appropriate use

Medical affairs processes should support scientific exchange, preserve appropriate separation from commercial activity, and make relevant field evidence usable by the teams responsible for evaluating it.

How to read this kit

  • Starter procedures are marked editable and require company review before operational use.
  • Dashboard data is synthetic and illustrates workflow rather than a real product or safety report.
  • Framework concepts are adaptable and should reflect local policy, system configuration, and applicable requirements.
