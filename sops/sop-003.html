Scientific Congress Insights Reporting Workflow

1. Purpose

To provide a repeatable process for planning, capturing, synthesizing, reviewing, and distributing scientific congress insights generated by Medical Affairs field teams.

2. Scope

Applies to MSLs, field medical leaders, and Medical Affairs operations staff participating in approved congress coverage or preparing an internal congress insights report. It does not authorize public communication or replace publication review requirements.

3. Responsibilities

  • Congress lead: define objectives, coverage, timelines, and report ownership.
  • MSL: capture observations with source context, distinguish data from interpretation, and submit within agreed timelines.
  • Medical Affairs reviewers: assess scientific accuracy, balance, privacy, and intended audience before internal circulation.
  • Operations: maintain templates, version control, distribution lists, and archive location.

4. Definitions

Congress insight
A structured observation about emerging evidence, scientific discussion, practice patterns, or unmet needs relevant to Medical Affairs.
Congress report
An internal, reviewed synthesis of selected congress observations and scientific updates.
Source verification
Confirmation that a reported claim is accurately represented by an authorized abstract, presentation, publication, or documented discussion.

5. Procedure

Plan congress coverage

  1. Set the scientific objectives and define therapeutic areas, sessions, and relevant coverage roles before the congress.
  2. Confirm current approved templates, expected submission dates, reviewer availability, and permitted internal audience.
  3. Remind participants of privacy, fair-balance, and source attribution requirements.

Capture observations

  1. Record session title, date, source type, and relevant abstract or publication identifier where available.
  2. Summarize the finding accurately, including relevant population, endpoint, and limitations when known. Avoid extrapolation beyond the presented evidence.
  3. Capture scientific questions or unmet needs as separate insight records in the approved system when applicable. Do not record patient-identifiable information.

Synthesize and review

  1. The report owner groups observations by scientific theme and removes duplicate entries while preserving traceability to sources.
  2. A qualified reviewer checks factual accuracy, context, balance, appropriate terminology, and audience suitability.
  3. Resolve reviewer comments and maintain a version history. Clearly label preliminary or embargoed information and observe applicable access restrictions.

Distribute and archive

  1. Distribute the approved report only to the authorized internal audience through an approved channel.
  2. Record final approval, distribution date, and archive location according to document control practice.
  3. Route actionable themes through the standard insight workflow and assign owners for any follow-up.

Conduct retrospective

  1. After the congress, review what was useful, coverage gaps, submission timeliness, and recurring themes.
  2. Update the planning checklist and template through the established document change process.

6. References

  1. Company congress coverage and scientific communications policies.
  2. Approved congress insight report template and document control procedure.
  3. Company medical insights collection and triage procedure.
  4. Applicable publication embargo, privacy, and information security requirements.
