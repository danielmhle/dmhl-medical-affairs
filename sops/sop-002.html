KOL Engagement Documentation Standards

A controlled-document format demonstration for field medical operations.

1. Purpose

To establish consistent documentation expectations for legitimate, non-promotional scientific engagements with external experts, including preparation, interaction records, and follow-up.

2. Scope

Applies to field medical personnel and Medical Affairs support colleagues documenting scientific interactions with healthcare professionals or other external experts in an approved CRM. Contracting, honoraria, grants, and event logistics remain governed by their respective procedures.

3. Responsibilities

  • MSL: ensure the scientific purpose is appropriate, prepare using approved resources, and document the interaction accurately and promptly.
  • Medical Affairs manager: provide coaching, review documentation quality, and address exceptions.
  • CRM process owner: maintain required fields, access controls, and retention configuration.
  • Compliance / Legal / Privacy partners: advise on applicable policy questions and escalation.

4. Definitions

KOL / external expert
A healthcare professional or other individual with relevant expertise who engages with Medical Affairs in a scientific capacity.
Scientific interaction
A documented, non-promotional exchange focused on scientific information, evidence, or medical practice.
CRM interaction record
The approved system record summarizing the purpose, attendees, content, and follow-up for an engagement.

5. Procedure

Confirm purpose and preparation

  1. Define the scientific objective and confirm that the proposed engagement is within the role and applicable policy.
  2. Review the expert’s relevant scientific interests and prior engagement context using approved sources and appropriate access.
  3. Prepare with current, approved scientific materials. Identify questions requiring medical information or other specialist support.

Conduct the engagement

  1. Clearly identify the Medical Affairs role and maintain a balanced, scientifically accurate discussion.
  2. Respond to unsolicited questions using approved information or arrange appropriate follow-up. Do not make commitments beyond authorized responsibilities.
  3. If an adverse event, product complaint, or privacy concern arises, follow the applicable escalation procedure promptly.

Document the interaction

  1. Enter the record in the approved CRM as soon as practicable and within the local documentation standard (illustrative target: two business days).
  2. Capture date, channel, attendees, scientific objective, topics discussed, key questions, materials shared, and agreed follow-up.
  3. Use factual, concise language. Distinguish the external expert’s stated views from the MSL’s assessment. Avoid sensitive personal data and subjective characterizations.

Complete follow-up and quality checks

  1. Assign follow-up actions to an owner and date. Provide requested scientific information through an approved channel and document completion.
  2. Link the interaction to the correct account and any associated insight record where supported by system configuration.
  3. Review the record for completeness, appropriate classification, and alignment with policy. Correct errors through the system’s audit-preserving process.

6. References

  1. Company policy on external expert interactions and scientific exchange.
  2. Company policy and procedures for transfers of value, contracting, and event activity.
  3. Approved CRM documentation guide.
  4. Applicable privacy, records retention, and adverse event reporting procedures.

Reference titles are representative. Confirm current controlled versions and local applicability before use.
